💚 It's small — and easy to fund

At these amounts, cost should never stop a school. A full AMC 8 for 30 students is only a few dollars per child. The bill can be covered by any of:

  • PTA / PTO — often the simplest path.
  • A local sponsor — a nearby business, credit union, or alum.
  • Crowdfunding — students, parents, teachers, or even strangers can chip in. It's a good cause: the money goes directly to help kids take a STEM exam.
