💡 You don't need the school to start. Many contests allow independent registration even if your child's school doesn't offer them — check the official rules and register directly (for example, Math Kangaroo), or find a nearby AMC test site at a college: AMC test sites at colleges. Focus on curiosity and fun first — competition second. Eligibility note: the MAA sets only a maximum grade and age for each AMC — AMC 8: grade 8 & under (under 15.5); AMC 10: grade 10 & under (under 17.5); AMC 12: grade 12 & under (under 19.5). No minimum age or grade is published, so a younger child can technically sit any of them — "Available" above means exactly that. Dates, fees, and rules change; verify them on the official sites.
