# Strategy notes: funding landscape & partners

_Researched 2026-11-24. All figures come from the public sources linked below. Re-verify them before quoting._

## Thesis
Contests measure talent; **math circles grow it**. The more parent-, teacher-, and
student-run circles a community has, the more students reach AMC → AIME → USAMO.
Jersey City today has **one known circle, and it is elementary-only**. Track
"new circles by grade level" as a core success metric, next to "new contest host schools."

## Partners

### American Institute of Mathematics (AIM): https://aimath.org
- One of NSF's six national math research institutes. It is at Caltech in Pasadena, with NSF and Fry Foundation support.
- Its **Joyful Mathematics** arm is the part relevant to us:
  - **Math Circle Network (MCN)**, https://mathcircles.org: free resources for all U.S. circles
    (student, teacher, family). It has a circle map, facilitator map, activities database, and
    organizer toolkits (planning, recruiting, facilitator guides, funding).
    - Grants: $500–$3,000 collaboration grants, reviewed quarterly (3/31, 6/30, 9/30, 11/15).
      There is also a New Circle Training & Mentoring cohort ($1,500 per circle plus $500 for a
      mentor visit). The 2026 round is closed; watch for the next one.
    - Contact: circles@aimath.org (offers startup meetings).
  - **Math Communities**, https://mathcommunities.org: a coalition of 12+ partner programs.
  - Replicable local models: SoCal Joyful Math Ecosystem, Morgan Hill Math.
- **Ask:** co-pilot in Jersey City. We identify neighborhoods and grade levels that have no circle,
  and they provide toolkits, a facilitator, and a startup grant.

### NSF Mathematical Sciences Research Institutes ("MSIP")
- Solicitation NSF 23-606: 5–8 awards, $2.5M–$6M per year each, 5-year continuing grants.
  It expects outreach "to the scientific community and the public at large" and a
  broadening-participation plan. The solicitation has no explicit K-12 language.
- Funded in Aug 2025 (over $74M total): AIM (Caltech), ICARM (CMU), ICERM (Brown),
  IMSI (UChicago consortium), IAS School of Mathematics, SLMath (Berkeley).
- **Ask:** use our map as a measurable public-outreach and broadening-participation channel
  for these institutes.

### Other NSF programs
- **CAMEL K-12 (NSF 26-501)**, co-funded with the Walton Family Foundation: research networks
  that build K-12 math datasets. Awards up to $1.5M over 3 years. Phase I deadlines have passed
  (LOI 1/9/2026, proposal 3/11/2026), and Phase II is open only to Phase I awardees. The best way
  in is to partner with a Phase I awardee and offer our school-level, NCES-keyed dataset.
  Eligible applicants: universities, education or research nonprofits, and Tribal Nations.
- **MATHINF (Mathematical Sciences Infrastructure Program, NSF 26-520)**: DMS infrastructure,
  training, and broad conferences. It is a weak fit for K-12 work.

## How NSF is funded
- NSF is a federal agency funded by **annual appropriations from Congress** (the
  Commerce-Justice-Science bill). It has no fee or endowment income.
- **FY2026 enacted: $8.75B**, 0.9% below FY2025 (P.L. 119-75). That is far below the $17.8B
  authorized level.
- **FY2027 President's request: $4.0B (a 55% cut).** It would cut the Mathematical and Physical
  Sciences directorate (which includes the Division of Mathematical Sciences, DMS) by 67%.
  Congress has not acted yet. In FY2026, Congress rejected a similarly deep proposed cut.
- **Implication:** do not depend on NSF money. Treat the NSF institutes as partners and channels
  (credibility, reach), not as our main funding source.

## How the AMC is funded (MAA economic model)
- The AMC is run by the **Mathematical Association of America**, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit
  (EIN 16-0743079).
- **Revenue comes from host-site fees, not student entry fees.** Schools and other hosts pay
  a registration fee ($55 early / $75 regular / $115 late) plus test bundles of 10 students
  (about $25 for AMC 8, $30 for AMC 10/12). For 2027, AIME takers pay $85 to Pearson.
- **Corporate sponsors** also contribute. For example, Jane Street is listed as a Diamond
  sponsor of the MAA. The AMC's full 2025 sponsor list is not confirmed.
- MAA financials from its IRS Form 990 filings (via ProPublica). "Program revenue" includes the competitions:

  | Year | Total revenue | Contributions | Program revenue | Investment | Expenses | Net assets |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 2024 | $7.98M | $3.51M | $4.89M | $1.12M | $10.18M | $25.6M |
  | 2023 | $8.60M | $2.75M | $4.12M | $0.65M | $10.05M | $24.0M |
  | 2022 | $8.99M | $4.35M | $2.99M | $0.89M | $9.86M | $22.3M |

- **Program revenue is growing** (+64% from 2022 to 2024). Reported expenses exceed reported
  revenue, but net assets still rose each year, which points to investment gains reported
  outside revenue.
