🗽 Jersey City Pilot — Math-Competition Registry

Every Jersey City school, and whether it hosts math contests. Data is incomplete on purpose — help us verify it. If your school runs AMC, MOEMS, MATHCOUNTS, Math League, or Math Kangaroo, click Verify / add exams — it opens a quick form (no account needed). 🏠 Home

Why this matters: the official published lists badly under-count real participation (e.g. OLC runs the AMC 8 but isn't listed anywhere). A trusted, complete registry is built from your knowledge + school outreach. Contact info (phone) below comes from the federal NCES directory.
🏆 Three champions per school: a school keeps hosting year after year when three people step up — a staff Competition Manager (registers and proctors), a parent champion (keeps it going, recruits families), and a student champion (spreads the word, helps run practice). The Champions column shows which schools still need one — volunteer for your school.
🤝 Math circle gap: Jersey City has just one known math circle, and it serves elementary students only. Middle and high schoolers have no local circle. Parents, teachers, and students can start one with free toolkits, grants, and mentoring from the Math Circle Network. See our 4-step guide, and if you run or start a circle, tell us.
