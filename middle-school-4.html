Why AMC 8 fits MS 4

  • The age is right: AMC 8 is for students in grade 8 and below — every MS 4 student is eligible.
  • The math base is already strong: 88.3% of MS 4 eighth graders scored proficient or above in Algebra I (2024–25 NJSLA), vs 66.6% district-wide.
  • The cost is small: $75 to register as a site, plus $25 per 10 students. One 40-minute session, once a year.
  • It is not a host site yet: MS 4 does not appear on the MAA's published AMC host list — that is exactly what this outreach is fixing.

How this page helps

  • For parents: see the plan, who is involved, and where it stands — then sign up below to help, instead of wondering whom to ask.
  • For the principal: a ready-made ask with the costs, the steps, and the deadline — not a cold call. Everything is on one page.
  • For students: AMC 8 is 25 questions in 40 minutes, for grades 8 and below — a low-stakes first taste of competition math. Try the practice test.
  • For the next school: a public tracker of real progress. When MS 4 registers, this page becomes the playbook other Jersey City schools can copy.
