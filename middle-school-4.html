Why AMC 8 fits MS 4

  • The age is right: AMC 8 is for students in grade 8 and below — every MS 4 student is eligible.
  • The math base is already strong: 88.3% of MS 4 eighth graders scored proficient or above in Algebra I (2024–25 NJSLA), vs 66.6% district-wide.
  • The cost is small: $75 to register as a site, plus $25 per 10 students. One 40-minute session, once a year.
  • It is not a host site yet: MS 4 does not appear on the MAA's published AMC host list — that is exactly what this outreach is fixing.

What the school needs to do — four steps

Registering is a one-time setup: one staff member, a few documents, and about a week. Here is the whole path, start to finish.

  1. Name a Competition Manager. One staff member — a math teacher, department chair, or the principal — registers the school and proctors the exam. That is the only staffing the school has to provide.
  2. Get vetted by MAA first. A new site must be vetted before it can register. Email the documents below to amcinfo@maa.org with the subject line “Vetting Documentation for [Your Name] and [Your Institution Name]”. Allow 3 business days — longer near deadlines, so send early.
    • A photo of a valid staff ID (school staff only) or a signed letter on school letterhead from the principal, verifying the Competition Manager and confirming the exam will be proctored in person at the school.
    • The school email address.
    • The school website — ps4.jcboe.org (a faculty page, if available).
    • A link to the school's listing on the NCES public school search (MS 4's NCES ID is 340783000539).
  3. Register and order bundles. Once MAA approves the vetting, the Registration button appears after logging in at the MAA Competition Portal. Register by Jan 5, 2027 ($75) and order student bundles ($25 per 10 students). More students can be added until Jan 26, 2027.
  4. Exam day. One 40-minute session, proctored in person at the school, on one day in the Jan 21–27, 2027 window.

Full detail, including the MAA's vetting requirements as shared in November 2026: guide.html — how a school hosts AMC. Fees and deadlines change — confirm them at maa.org/amcreg before paying.

What it costs — sized for MS 4

Two charges, both from MAA, and nothing else. The $75 is a one-time site fee for the year; bundles are $25 per 10 students. Practice material on this site is free.

  • Start small, add later: extra bundles can be ordered until Jan 26, 2027, so the school can register 20 students now and add more as word spreads.
  • Whole school: all 755 students would need 76 bundles (75.5 rounds up, since bundles come in tens) — $1,975 total, about $2.62 per student. That is the ceiling; the first year is usually a smaller group.
  • What the fee covers: the site registration and the student exam licenses. There is no other MAA fee — no per-school surcharge, no proctor fee. The exam is a single 40-minute session run by the school's own staff.
  • Deadlines that matter: register by Jan 5, 2027 at the $75 rate — that is the final deadline for new sites. (The $55 early-bird rate ended Oct 28, 2026; the $115 late rate from Jan 14, 2027 is for returning sites only.)

Who pays for it

The whole first year is $125–$200 — small enough that no single funder has to carry it. Four ways it could be covered:

  • Parents for 4 (the MS 4 PTA) — the first ask. The school's parent organization is called Parents for 4 — the MS 4 Parent Teacher Organization / Parent Council. It exists to support the school, get parents involved, and keep parents, teachers, and the administration talking to each other. It meets monthly, typically Tuesdays 6:00–6:30 PM, in person and virtual, with a member of the administration usually there and time for questions. Any parent, guardian, or adult acting as a parent can join. But it has not been asked yet, and whether it supports AMC is unknown — to confirm, not assumed. The first step is raising it at a PTA meeting. Reach them at parentsfor4@gmail.com, on Facebook at facebook.com/parentsfor4, or via their signup form (grade WhatsApp groups and email list). Officers as of 2024–25 — they may have rotated since: President Marybeth Bufka Donaldson; Vice Presidents Shabina Patel and Jennie Pu; Secretary Doria Ceraso; Treasurer Sapna Tanna. A parent champion is already on board to help carry the ask.
  • Parents. At 30 students, $150 is $5 per student — a handful of families could split it, or a small per-student contribution covers the year.
  • School budget. The principal can cover a one-time $125–$200 from the school's budget.
  • A local sponsor. A Jersey City business or community donor could cover the whole first year for $125–$200.

Our ask of the principal is only a conversation — the money is small enough that the PTA, parents, or a single sponsor can cover it. Nobody is being asked to commit funds on this page.
